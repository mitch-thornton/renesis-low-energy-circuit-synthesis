circuit description files
Reports switched-capacitance energy (cv2_cycle_pJ) and writes a dual-rail .tgn. Every field maps to a CLI flag or --option in RENESIS-MANUAL.md.
This pipeline differs from the quantum tool by design: scratch is LEFT DIRTY (uncomputation costs more switching energy than the Landauer erasure it avoids, measured crossover ≈ half of kT ln2), wide gates cost linear not exponential, and the cover is priced by expected switched capacitance. Width is deliberately not minimised. See ADIABATIC-VS-QUANTUM.md.
